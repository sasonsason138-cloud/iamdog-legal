I AM DOG — Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 20, 2026

I AM DOG ("the app", "we", "us") is a 3D game where you play as a dog. This page explains what information the app collects and how it is used.

Your account (optional)

You can play as a guest: then your progress (bones, levels, outfits, settings) stays only on your device. If you choose to create an account (email and password, or Google sign-in), we store your email address, a unique account ID and a copy of your game progress in Google Firebase (Authentication and Cloud Firestore) so you can keep playing on another phone. Only you can read or change your saved progress.

Advertising

The free version shows ads through Google AdMob. AdMob may collect device identifiers (such as the advertising ID), approximate location derived from your IP address, and information about ad interactions in order to show, limit and measure ads, as described in Google's Partner Sites policy. Where required by law (for example in the EEA and UK), the app asks for your consent first, and you can change your choice at any time from Settings → "Ad privacy". You can also reset or limit ad tracking in your device's system settings.

In-app purchases

Optional purchases (such as removing ads or bone packs) are processed entirely by Google Play. We never see or store your payment details. We only record on your device which items you own.

Notifications

If you allow notifications, the app schedules local reminders on your device. No data is sent to us for this.

Children

The app is not directed to children under 13 and we do not knowingly collect personal information from children.

What we don't do

We don't sell your data. We don't collect your contacts, photos, precise location or any data beyond what is described above.

Deleting your account and data

In the app open Settings → Account → Delete account: your account and cloud save are deleted immediately. You can also ask us to delete them by emailing sasonsason138@gmail.com from the address you signed up with; we delete the account and all its data within 30 days. Progress kept only on your device is removed with Settings → "Reset progress" or by uninstalling the app. For advertising data, see Google's controls at myadcenter.google.com.

Contact

Questions about this policy: sasonsason138@gmail.com

I AM DOG — מדיניות פרטיות

אפשר לשחק כאורח, ואז ההתקדמות נשמרת רק במכשיר. אם יוצרים חשבון (אימייל וסיסמה או Google), נשמרים כתובת האימייל, מזהה החשבון ועותק של ההתקדמות ב-Google Firebase, כדי שתוכל להמשיך בטלפון אחר.

פרסומות: הגרסה החינמית מציגה פרסומות של Google AdMob, שעשויה לאסוף מזהי מכשיר (כמו מזהה הפרסום) ומידע על אינטראקציה עם פרסומות כדי להציג ולמדוד אותן. במקומות שהחוק מחייב, תתבקש הסכמה מראש, ותוכל לשנות אותה בכל עת בהגדרות → "פרטיות פרסומות".

רכישות: רכישות מתבצעות דרך Google Play בלבד. איננו רואים ואיננו שומרים פרטי תשלום.

התראות: תזכורות מקומיות בלבד, במכשיר שלך.

מחיקת חשבון ומידע: הגדרות → חשבון → "מחיקת חשבון" (מיידי), או בקשה במייל ל-sasonsason138@gmail.com (תוך 30 יום).
