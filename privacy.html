I AM DOG — Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 19, 2026

I AM DOG ("the app", "we", "us") is a 3D game where you play as a dog. This page explains what information the app collects and how it is used.

No account, no personal profile

The app has no sign-up or login. Your progress (bones, levels, outfits, settings) is stored only on your device. Uninstalling the app deletes it.

Advertising

The free version shows ads through Google AdMob. AdMob may collect device identifiers (such as the advertising ID), approximate location derived from your IP address, and information about ad interactions in order to show, limit and measure ads, as described in Google's Partner Sites policy. Where required by law (for example in the EEA and UK), the app asks for your consent first, and you can change your choice at any time from Settings → "Ad privacy". You can also reset or limit ad tracking in your device's system settings.

In-app purchases

Optional purchases (such as removing ads or bone packs) are processed entirely by Google Play. We never see or store your payment details. We only record on your device which items you own.

Notifications

If you allow notifications, the app schedules local reminders on your device. No data is sent to us for this.

Children

The app is not directed to children under 13 and we do not knowingly collect personal information from children.

What we don't do

We don't sell your data. We don't collect your name, email, contacts, photos, precise location or any data beyond what is described above.

Data deletion

All game data lives on your device: use Settings → "Reset progress" in the app, or uninstall it. For advertising data, see Google's controls at myadcenter.google.com.
